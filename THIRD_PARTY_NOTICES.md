KeyFoley includes processed selections from these original 48 kHz, 24-bit
stereo WAV groups:

- `keyboard scissor switch single key 1.wav` through `20.wav`
- `keyboard mechanical single key 1.wav` through `14.wav`
- `typewriter single letter 1.wav` through `18.wav`
- `typewriter spacebar 1.wav` through `3.wav`
- `typewriter next line 1.wav` through `4.wav`
- `mechanical pencil click 1.wav` through `8.wav`
- `pen click extend 1.wav` through `5.wav`
- `pen click retract 1.wav` through `5.wav`
- `pen cap removed 1.wav` through `4.wav`
- `pen cap replaced 1.wav` through `4.wav`
- `pencil writing quick dash 1.wav` through `17.wav`

The included versions have leading silence trimmed, short fades applied, been
downmixed to mono, peak-normalized to approximately −3 dB, and converted to
48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. They provide the `剪刀脚`, `机械键盘`,
`经典打字机`, `桌面点击`, and `铅笔笔记` themes.

- Creator: Kenney

- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

The 100 original OGG files are included in the independent sound asset

## Mechvibes keyboard sounds

- Creator and copyright: Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen
- Source: [Mechvibes](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes/tree/main/src/audio)
- License: [MIT License](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes/blob/main/LICENSE)

KeyFoley includes the following Mechvibes sound packs:

- `CherryMX Blue - ABS keycaps` as the `青轴 · ABS` built-in theme
- `CherryMX Black - PBT keycaps` as the `黑轴 · PBT` built-in theme
- `CherryMX Black - ABS keycaps` in the independent sound asset library
- `CherryMX Blue - PBT keycaps` in the independent sound asset library

Each source pack stores many short recordings in one OGG audio sprite.
KeyFoley splits the sprite using its accompanying `config.json`, downmixes it
to mono, and converts the result to 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. The MIT copyright
and permission notice above must remain with redistributed copies.
